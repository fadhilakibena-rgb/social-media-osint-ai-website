Privacy Policy

Social Media OSINT AI Research

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Purpose

Social Media OSINT AI Research is a research-oriented platform for examining social media trends, public discussions, sentiment, and emerging issues.

2. Information Collected

Depending on available permissions, the application may process publicly available content and information provided by users who explicitly authorize access, including video metadata, captions, publication dates, and engagement information.

3. Use of Information

Information is used for descriptive research, trend analysis, and development of analytical tools. Automated findings are subject to human verification.

4. Data Access

Access to platform data is subject to applicable API permissions, platform policies, and legal requirements.

5. Data Protection

The project aims to restrict access to collected data to authorized personnel and protect authentication credentials against unauthorized disclosure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data should be retained only as necessary for legitimate research purposes and applicable legal or platform requirements. Requests concerning authorized account data will be assessed and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Third-Party Platforms

The application may integrate with services such as TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram. Each platform operates under its own privacy policies.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices or applicable requirements change.
