Social Media OSINT AI Research

About the Project

Social Media OSINT AI Research is a research-oriented platform for analyzing publicly available and appropriately authorized social media information.

Research Objectives

The project explores social media trends, temporal patterns, public discussions, sentiment, and emerging issues using descriptive analytics and artificial intelligence.

Supported Platforms

The platform is being developed to support YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Telegram, and Reddit, subject to applicable permissions and API availability.

Privacy and Responsible Use

The project aims to respect platform policies, applicable privacy requirements, and authorized data-access limitations. Analytical findings require human verification.
